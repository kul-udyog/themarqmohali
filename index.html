Overview

Gather your assets, for the biggest asset of your life.

The Marq by Atlantis is a new address taking shape on PR-7 Airport Road, Block-B, GMADA Aerocity, Sector 82, Mohali — conceived as a landmark for those who measure a home by its presence, not just its plan. Every detail of the identity, from the prowling mark to the name itself, is built around a single idea: a residence that commands the skyline it sits in.

Set across an approximate 3.4 acres directly on the Airport Road corridor, The Marq sits among the most closely watched addresses in the Tricity region — Premium Residences, Contemporary Architecture, designed for a lifestyle that doesn't ask for attention. It holds it.

Landscaped Podium & Water Feature

A resort-style landscaped podium threads between the four towers, anchored by a central water feature — the shared green core of The Marq.

Tennis Courts

Dedicated tennis courts sit within the podium landscaping, a short walk from Tower 1.

Double-Height Clubhouse & Lounge

A double-height clubhouse and lounge anchor the podium level, designed as the address's social heart.

Statement Tower Lobbies

Every tower opens into a considered arrival experience — from Tower 1's double-height lobby to Tower 3's triple-height entrance hall.

Internal Retail High Street

Independent Shop-Cum-Office (SCO) units run across basement, ground, first, and second floors — everyday retail at the base of the towers.

EV Charging & Rainwater Harvesting

In line with GMADA's approved norms for the site, provision is made for electric-vehicle charging points and rooftop rainwater harvesting.
