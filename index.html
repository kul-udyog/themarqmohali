Overview

Gather your assets, for the biggest asset of your life.

The Marq by Atlantis is a new address taking shape on PR-7 Airport Road, Block-B, GMADA Aerocity, Sector 82, Mohali — conceived as a landmark for those who measure a home by its presence, not just its plan. Every detail of the identity, from the prowling mark to the name itself, is built around a single idea: a residence that commands the skyline it sits in.

Set across an approximate 3.4 acres directly on the Airport Road corridor, The Marq sits among the most closely watched addresses in the Tricity region — Premium Residences, Contemporary Architecture, designed for a lifestyle that doesn't ask for attention. It holds it.
