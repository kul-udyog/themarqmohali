Configurations & Floor Plans: Which Tower Has Which Unit at The Marq

The Marq by Atlantis isn't one tower with four unit types thrown together — each tower is built around a specific configuration, with a different number of units clustered per core. Here's the tower-by-tower breakdown, sizes, and cluster layouts, drawn directly from the project's approved unit plans.

The four towers at a glance

Tower Configurations Sizes Units per core
Tower 1 3 BHK & 4 BHK 2,525 & 2,850 sq.ft. 4 units to a core
Tower 2 4+1 BHK 3,150 sq.ft. 2 units to a core
Tower 3 3 BHK & 4 BHK 2,525 & 2,850 sq.ft. 4 units to a core
Tower 4 4+1 BHK & 5+1 BHK 3,150 & 5,050 sq.ft. Mixed — see below
Every tower shares the same base: G+22 rising around a landscaped central podium, with double basement parking underneath. What differs tower to tower is the unit mix and how many homes share each lift-and-stair core — which is the detail most brochures skip.

Tower 1 & Tower 3 — 3 BHK and 4 BHK

3 BHK — 2,525 sq.ft.

Available in Tower 1 and Tower 3

The entry configuration at The Marq, offered in two layout variants (Type-1 and Type-2) within the same footprint. Four units — a mix of 3 BHK and 4 BHK — share each floor's core in these towers.

4 BHK — 2,850 sq.ft.

Available in Tower 1 and Tower 3

Sits in the same towers and same four-to-a-core cluster as the 3 BHK, also offered in two layout variants (Type-2 and Type-3), giving buyers a choice of unit orientation within the tower.

Tower 2 — 4+1 BHK

4+1 BHK — 3,150 sq.ft.

Available in Tower 2 (and Tower 4 — see below)

Tower 2 is dedicated entirely to the 4+1 BHK configuration, with just two units sharing each core instead of four. That lower unit-per-core count is the main structural difference between Tower 2 and Towers 1 & 3 — fewer neighbours per landing, for the same building height.

Tower 4 — 4+1 BHK and 5+1 BHK

4+1 BHK — 3,150 sq.ft.

Available in Tower 4 (two further layout variants, Type-4 and Type-5)

Tower 4 carries its own pair of 4+1 BHK layout variants alongside the tower's flagship 5+1 BHK homes — so the 4+1 configuration exists in two different towers (2 and 4), each with its own layout options.

5+1 BHK — 5,050 sq.ft.

Available only in Tower 4

The largest and most exclusive configuration at The Marq, offered in two layout variants (Type-1 and Type-2). This is the only tower where the 5+1 BHK is available.

What's common across every tower

  • Height: all four towers rise G+22 around a shared landscaped podium.
  • Parking: double basement parking runs underneath the full site, shared across towers.
  • Ground floor: stilt/ground floor parking plus a double-height club lounge, with SCO (G+2) retail fronting the internal high street — see the amenities for the full list.
  • Entry: each tower has its own dedicated entrance lobby at basement and ground level.

Frequently asked

Which tower is the 5+1 BHK in?
Only Tower 4. It's not offered in Tower 1, 2 or 3.

How many units share a floor?
Tower 1 and Tower 3 cluster four units per core (a 3 BHK / 4 BHK mix); Tower 2 clusters just two 4+1 BHK units per core, giving those floors fewer neighbours per landing.

Is the 4+1 BHK the same layout in Tower 2 and Tower 4?
No — Tower 2 has its own 4+1 BHK layout, while Tower 4 offers two further 4+1 BHK layout variants in addition to the 5+1 BHK. All are 3,150 sq.ft., but the internal layouts differ by tower.
